Our Story

StickerNest started as a small idea in the streets of Kathmandu — turning the city's colors, culture, and chaos into stickers you can carry anywhere. Every design on this site is made by a local artist, hand-drawn and digitized with love for the details that make Kathmandu, Kathmandu.

Locally Made

Every sticker is designed by an independent artist based in Kathmandu.

Supporting Artists

A share of every purchase goes directly back to the artist who made it.

Made with Love

No mass production — just original art, printed and shipped with care.

Rooted in Kathmandu

Inspired by the streets, temples, and everyday life of the city we call home.

Meet Our Artists

View all artists
